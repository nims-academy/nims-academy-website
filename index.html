How to Apply

  1. Explore CoursesReview the courses offered.
  2. Check EligibilityConfirm the applicable course requirements with the institute.
  3. Prepare DocumentsGather the required certificates and photographs.
  4. Contact AdmissionsCall or WhatsApp the admissions office for guidance.

Document checklist

  • SSLC certificate
  • Plus Two certificate
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Aadhaar copy
  • Passport-size photographs

Eligibility

  • Age limit: 17–35 years
  • Minimum 40%–45% in Class 10 and Class 12, as applicable
